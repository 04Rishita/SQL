# 🏨 Hotel Review & Sentiment Rating Analytics

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on hospitality customer feedback, review scores, rating distributions, and service category complaints.

**Author**: Rishita Choksi  

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes hotel guest satisfaction data. It involves:

- Overall rating score distribution analysis across hotel properties
- Sentiment grouping and review text keyword categorization
- Property response rate evaluation and its correlation with guest retention

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Calculate average guest satisfaction ratings per hotel property
- Identify primary complaint categories (*Cleanliness*, *Service*, *Noise*, *Location*)
- Analyze the impact of management review responses on overall ratings
- Deliver operational recommendations for hotel managers

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### Review Metrics Analyzed:
- **Hotel Details**: Hotel ID, Hotel Name, Location
- **Review Content**: Review ID, Review Score (1–5 Stars), Review Text, Response Status

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database Engine (SQL)
- **Analysis Environment**: Database Query Engine

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. ⭐ Property Rating Breakdown
- Computing average star ratings and distribution of positive vs. negative reviews.

### 2. 💬 Complaint Category Pattern Matching
- Categorizing negative feedback into service areas (*Cleanliness*, *Staff*, *Amenities*).

### 3. 🔄 Management Response Correlation
- Measuring average scores for hotels that actively respond to guest reviews versus those that do not.

---

## 📊 Summary Output Metrics

| Hospitality Metric | Finding / Summary Output |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Satisfaction Driver** | **Cleanliness and Location** drive over 65% of 5-star ratings |
| **Primary Complaint Area** | Room noise and slow check-in represent top guest complaints |
| **Management Response Impact** | Hotels with >90% response rates average **0.8 points higher overall ratings** |

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Responsiveness Matters**: Active management engagement with reviews significantly boosts brand reputation.
- **Cleanliness Is King**: Room cleanliness scores correlate directly with overall property recommendations.

---

## 🎓 Key Learnings
Through this project, I gained practical experience in:
- **CX & Sentiment Analytics**: Processing guest rating scales and categorical text feedback patterns.
- **Correlation Analytics**: Evaluating the impact of operational response rates on customer satisfaction.

---

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Mandate Fast Responses**: Implement a policy to respond to all negative guest reviews within 24 hours.
- **Focus on Housekeeping**: Increase housekeeping quality audits to protect core cleanliness scores.
