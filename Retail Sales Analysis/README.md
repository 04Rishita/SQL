# 🛍️ Retail Customer Behavior & Sales Segmentation

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on retail transaction data. This analysis focuses on data sanitization, customer demographic profiling, spending patterns by age group, category performance, and time-of-day transaction shifts to optimize targeted marketing and store layout.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project cleans and analyzes retail customer purchase records. It involves:

- Data sanitization and handling of null or incomplete transaction records
- Customer demographic segmentation by gender and age brackets
- Category-wise sales volume and revenue evaluation
- Time-of-day purchase shift categorization (Morning, Afternoon, Evening shifts)

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Perform data hygiene by identifying and removing null or corrupted records
- Evaluate sales performance across product categories (*Clothing*, *Beauty*, *Electronics*)
- Analyze customer demographics to identify the highest spending age brackets
- Segment transaction patterns by time of day to optimize shift management
- Deliver strategic recommendations for inventory stocking and targeted advertising

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### `SQL - Retail Sales Analysis_utf .csv`
- **Customer Metrics**: Transaction ID, Customer ID, Gender, Age
- **Transaction Details**: Sale Date, Sale Time, Product Category, Quantity Sold
- **Financial Details**: Unit Price ($), Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Total Sale Amount ($)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database System (SQL / MySQL)
- **Analysis Environment**: Database Workbench

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. 🧹 Data Hygiene & Quality Control
- Identifying missing values across key fields and executing sanitization protocols.

### 2. 👥 Demographic & Age Group Segmentation
- Grouping customers into age brackets to measure spending habits and purchasing frequency across genders.

### 3. 🛍️ Category Revenue Breakdown
- Analyzing revenue generation and unit sales across core product categories (*Clothing*, *Beauty*, *Electronics*).

### 4. ⏰ Shift & Time-of-Day Transaction Analysis
- Categorizing sales time slots into *Morning*, *Afternoon*, and *Evening* shifts to track hourly store traffic.

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Core Demographic Target**: Adults aged 30–45 contribute over 40% of total retail store revenue.
- **Category Popularity**: Clothing leads in overall sales volume, while Beauty products yield high average customer frequency.
- **Afternoon Traffic Peak**: Store footfall and online order checkouts peak during afternoon hours.
