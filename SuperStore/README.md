# 🏬 SuperStore Profitability & Discount Impact Analysis

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on retail enterprise sales, shipping efficiency, and discount structures. This project analyzes profit margins across product categories (*Furniture*, *Office Supplies*, *Technology*), regional zones, and corporate customer segments to identify loss-making discount thresholds.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates retail supply chain profitability. It involves:

- Sales vs. Profit margin analysis across product categories and sub-categories
- Discount impact assessment to determine profitability erosion thresholds
- Regional profitability performance comparisons
- Shipping mode cost-benefit evaluation

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Determine net profit margins across product categories and regions
- Analyze the correlation between high discounts (>20%) and negative profit margins
- Identify loss-making product sub-categories
- Deliver actionable recommendations for discount capping and shipping optimization

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### Dataset Columns Analyzed:
- **Order Metadata**: Order ID, Order Date, Ship Mode, Customer Segment
- **Product Details**: Category (*Furniture*, *Office Supplies*, *Technology*), Sub-Category
- **Financial Metrics**: Sales ($), Quantity, Discount (%), Net Profit ($)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database Engine (SQL)
- **Analysis Tools**: Database Query Engine

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. 📈 Category Profitability
- Measuring net sales against gross profit margins across Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies.

### 2. ⚠️ Discount Erosion Assessment
- Evaluating how steep discounting impacts final net bottom-line profitability.

### 3. 🌎 Regional Performance Ranking
- Comparing profit performance across East, West, Central, and South sales regions.

---

## 📊 Summary Output Metrics

| Dimension / Metric | Finding / Summary Output |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Profit Category** | **Technology** yields the highest profit margin (~18%) |
| **Loss-Making Category** | **Furniture (specifically Tables)** suffers negative margins under heavy discounts |
| **Discount Threshold Hazard** | Discounts exceeding **20%** consistently lead to net financial losses |
| **Top Regional Margin** | **West Region** leads overall network profitability |

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Discount Destroys Margins**: Heavy discounting on Furniture items creates sales volume without generating net profit.
- **Technology Delivers Profit**: High-tech accessories maintain strong margins even when discounted moderately.

---

## 🎓 Key Learnings
Through this project, I gained practical experience in:
- **Profit Margin Formulations**: Formulating margin ratios and net bottom-line metrics in SQL.
- **Financial Threshold Analysis**: Identifying operational break-even points for promotional discounts.

---

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Cap Promotional Discounts**: Cap discounts at a maximum of 15–20% on Furniture items.
- **Promote Tech Accessories**: Focus corporate sales efforts on high-margin Technology bundles.
