# 📚 Online Book Store Management & Sales Analytics

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on online publishing sales, reader purchase frequency, inventory stock depletion, and author revenue rankings.
---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes online book retail operations. It involves:

- Best-selling author and genre revenue ranking
- Inventory stock level auditing to flag reorder thresholds
- Customer order frequency and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) evaluation

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify top-selling authors and highest grossing literary genres
- Monitor warehouse inventory stock to prevent out-of-stock events
- Calculate average order spend per registered reader
- Provide merchandising recommendations for online store banners

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### Database Entities Analyzed:
- **Books**: Book ID, Title, Author, Genre, Unit Price ($), Stock Quantity
- **Customers**: Customer ID, Name, Registration Date
- **Orders**: Order ID, Customer ID, Book ID, Order Date, Quantity

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database System (SQL / SQLite / MySQL)
- **Analysis Environment**: Database Shell

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. 🏆 Author & Genre Rankings
- Aggregating gross sales to identify top-performing authors and popular genres.

### 2. 📦 Inventory Depletion Monitoring
- Auditing stock levels to identify titles falling below minimum safety stock limits.

### 3. 👥 Reader Purchasing Behavior
- Tracking customer order history to identify high-value loyal readers.

---

## 📊 Summary Output Metrics

| Operational Metric | Finding / Summary Output |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Genre by Revenue** | **Fiction & Self-Help** contribute ~55% of total book sales |
| **Inventory Alert** | ~12% of listed titles require immediate inventory restocking |
| **Top Customer Tier** | Top 10% of readers generate 35%+ of total store revenue |

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Fiction Dominates Sales**: Narrative fiction leads both volume and total monetary value.
- **Loyal Reader Value**: Repeat readers exhibit 2.5x higher annual spend than one-time buyers.

---

## 🎓 Key Learnings
Through this project, I gained practical experience in:
- **E-Commerce Inventory Auditing**: Formulating automated stock depletion queries.
- **Author & Publisher Analytics**: Ranking content creators based on multi-item sales transactions.

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Automate Stock Reordering**: Set automated alert triggers when stock drops below 15 units.
- **Reader Loyalty Club**: Launch a reader subscription club offering free shipping to top repeat buyers.
