# ⚡ EV Charging Station Network & Utilization Analytics

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on Electric Vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure, power delivery metrics (kWh), station utilization rates, and revenue streams across commercial and residential locations.
---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates clean energy charging network efficiency. It involves:

- Peak charging hour identification across urban networks
- Power consumption tracking (kWh) by plug type (*DC Fast Charger* vs. *Standard AC Charger*)
- Location-wise utilization rate calculations
- Revenue generation analysis across commercial vs. residential sites

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify peak charging hours and network congestion windows
- Measure total energy delivered (kWh) per charging session
- Compare financial returns of Fast DC Chargers versus Standard AC Chargers
- Deliver grid load management and infrastructure expansion recommendations

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### Data Metrics Analyzed:
- **Station Metrics**: Station ID, Location Type (*Commercial*, *Residential*, *Highway*)
- **Session Details**: Session ID, Plug Type, Start Time, End Time, Duration
- **Power & Revenue**: Energy Delivered (kWh), Charging Fee ($)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database System (SQL / MySQL)
- **Analysis Environment**: Database Workbench

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. ⏰ Peak Utilization Hours
- Tracking hourly station occupancy to identify grid bottleneck windows.

### 2. ⚡ Energy Delivery (kWh) by Charger Type
- Comparing power output efficiency between Fast Chargers and Standard Plugs.

### 3. 💵 Revenue Stream Analysis
- Evaluating income generated across location types and pricing tiers.

---

## 📊 Summary Output Metrics

| Network Metric | Finding / Summary Output |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Peak Utilization Window** | Morning hours (**8:00 AM – 11:00 AM**) experience 85%+ station occupancy |
| **Revenue Leader** | **DC Fast Chargers** contribute ~68% of total network revenue |
| **Top Location Type** | **Commercial Hubs** generate 2.5x higher revenue than Residential Plugs |

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Morning Bottlenecks**: Commercial stations face major congestion during morning office arrival hours.
- **Fast Chargers Drive Income**: Drivers willingly pay premium rates for high-speed charging.

---

## 🎓 Key Learnings
Through this project, I gained practical experience in:
- **IoT & Energy Analytics**: Processing timestamped operational sessions and power consumption figures.
- **Duration & Rate Metrics**: Calculating utilization percentages from raw connection timestamps.

---

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Implement Dynamic Pricing**: Introduce peak-hour surge pricing to incentivize off-peak charging.
- **Expand DC Fast Chargers**: Prioritize installing DC Fast Chargers at commercial highway stops.
