# ☕ Monday Coffee Expansion & Sales Strategy

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on coffee retail sales, city demographics, and consumer purchasing power. This analysis evaluates city-level coffee consumption, revenue per customer, and market saturation to identify optimal expansion targets for new Monday Coffee store locations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project evaluates market expansion opportunities for a coffee retail chain. It involves:

- City-level population and coffee consumer density evaluation
- Revenue and order volume tracking across existing store locations
- Customer spending power and average order spend analysis
- Strategic ranking of top target cities for retail store launches

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Rank cities by total coffee product sales revenue
- Calculate average spend per customer across different metropolitan areas
- Evaluate market saturation vs. coffee consumer population density
- Recommend top 3 priority cities for Monday Coffee store expansion

---

## 📁 Dataset & Files Included

### Data Entities Analyzed:
- **City Metrics**: City Name, Population, Estimated Coffee Consumers
- **Sales Transactions**: Transaction ID, Customer ID, City, Product Name, Order Amount ($)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Engine**: Relational Database Management System (SQL)
- **Analysis Tools**: Database Workbench

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Topics Covered

### 1. 🏙️ City-Wise Revenue Ranking
- Aggregating transaction metrics to rank cities by total gross sales.

### 2. ☕ Customer Spending & Basket Size
- Calculating average spending per customer to identify high-value consumer markets.

### 3. 🎯 Expansion Target Identification
- Combining population statistics and spend metrics to identify underserved coffee markets.

---

## 📊 Summary Output Metrics

| Expansion Metric | Top Target Finding |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Target Market #1** | **Pune** demonstrates highest per-capita spend and growth potential |
| **Top Target Market #2** | **Bangalore** leads in total coffee volume and repeat order rates |
| **Preferred Product Segment** | Ground coffee beans and specialty espresso yield highest profit margins |

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- **High-Demand Metro Hubs**: Major metropolitan hubs demonstrate strong coffee drinking culture and high average order spend.
- **Repeat Purchase Behavior**: Markets with higher tech-worker demographics exhibit 2x higher weekly repeat purchase rates.

---

## 🎓 Key Learnings
Through this project, I gained practical experience in:
- **Market Expansion Analytics**: Combining external demographic data with internal sales metrics to inform corporate expansion.
- **Location-Based Performance Metrics**: Computing per-capita spend and market penetration ratios across cities.

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Prioritize Store Openings**: Launch flagship Monday Coffee stores in Pune and Bangalore.
- **Target Commercial Districts**: Locate new stores near major tech parks and commercial business districts.
